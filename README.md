### 👨‍🔧 Whoami

In my 40s, I might carry the rough, weathered exterior of a hands-on techie, yet beneath it lies a sentimental soul. Above all else, I am a son, a husband, and a father—constantly striving to better myself every single day for the ones I love.

Coming of age during the turbulent transition between the **20th and 21st centuries** shaped my work ethic: disciplined, respectful of foundational values, yet endlessly curious about the shifting tides of time. To me, **learning is infinite**—not a day goes by without exploring, tinkering, and embracing something new.

* 💻 **Craft:** Semi-pro Frontend Developer.
* 🏛️ **Technical Roots & Evolution:** Forged in the golden era of classic web forums and server management (**vBulletin, WordPress, PHP, LAMP/LEMP stack**). Today, I am actively migrating towards modern architectures with **Payload CMS**, while expanding my horizons into **Go** and **Flutter**.
* 🌌 **Inner World:** An **existentialist** dreamer. I find quietude stargazing into the cosmos, immersing myself in music, poetry, and classic literature—especially **pre-1945** works.
* 🧪 **Olfactory Nostalgia:** An avid fragrance collector with a deep devotion to **vintage perfumes (pre-1999)**—where real oakmoss, rich resins, and uncompromising artistry still reigned supreme.
* 🧭 **Core Principles:** Unwavering respect for **Science, Truth, and Nature**. Always standing on the side of freedom and justice (`#StandWithUkraine` 🇺🇦).
